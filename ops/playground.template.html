2 · Batches

Each batch's Max Miles comes from its length. Rewards are priced as a share of it (section 3), so every batch has the full ladder. Price level 1.0 means the ladder exactly as written; a higher level makes everyday rewards cost more Miles, which keeps low-fee batches within budget. Type a level to lock it.

3 · Reward ladder

A reward costs % of the batch's Max Miles (rounded). Fixed rewards keep that percentage in every batch (a founder connect at 90% is 90% everywhere); the others are scaled by the batch's price level.
