Cost per batch

Miles = the Miles those redemptions use up. Low = every student who can claim takes the cheapest paid reward. High = each takes the costliest reward they can afford, once. Worst case = each spends all Miles on the costliest mix, repeating repeatable rewards up to the limit chosen. All three are multiplied by the share who redeem. Revenue = fee per student × students in the batch (edit the fee in the table).
