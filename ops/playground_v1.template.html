4 · Rewards: Miles needed and cost to Masai

Use − / + or type a value. ₹ per 1,000 Miles shows how expensive a reward is to Masai for the Miles it uses: red = over ₹500 per 1,000 Miles, which is where cost runs away when repeats are allowed. A cost of 0 adds nothing. ? = cost not confirmed yet, counted as 0.

5 · Batch rate cards

Weekly pattern:
How it works. Each batch's maximum possible Miles comes from its length: weeks × (2 mandatory live + 3 optional live + 4 practice items) + the evaluations. The target maximum is target% × fee ÷ (cost conversion × engagement × ₹ per Mile). The multiplier k is target ÷ standard maximum, and every rate in that batch is the standard rate × k, rounded to 5. Type a multiplier to lock it for a batch (a manual tag appears); weeks come from batch_design.csv and are editable. Yellow = changed from the default.
